# The Easy Way to Quit YouTube Addiction

An open-source guide to breaking free from Youtube Addiction.

## About The Book

The Easy Way to Quit YouTube Addiction applies the psychological principles of the Easyway/EasyPeasy method to break the habit of compulsive video consumption—including endless feeds, autoplay loops, video essays, shorts, and background streaming.

This book is not an attack on YouTube or video content. Videos are a normal and healthy part of modern media. This book addresses the moment entertainment quietly turns into emotional dependence—using digital noise to run away from boredom, stress, anxiety, or reality.

Read the chapters in order, and do not force yourself to quit watching YouTube until you finish the book.
